Do not infer uniform plan availability from public help. In particular, verify
recruiting source/quotas and incentive handling, skip-logic configuration, instruction
placement/enforcement, task capture, translations, accessibility, metadata and
export fields in the authorized environment. Retention, deletion, regional
processing, access and training use require applicable agreement/policy checks;
these pages do not establish them.

Do not invent API/MCP support or browser automation. No integration workflow was
verified for this skill. A manual runbook remains useful without an execution tool.

## Methodological recommendations

| ID / source | Guidance to apply | Scope and cautions |
|---|---|---|
| **M1** [Government Analysis Function: Remote testing survey questions](https://analysisfunction.civilservice.gov.uk/policy-store/remote-testing-survey-questions/) | Plan tasks, test technology, run mock sessions, reflect recording in consent, assess devices, assistive technology and privacy. Choose methods for aims and participant needs. | Published 2020; discussion of predetermined unmoderated follow-ups is not a description of current adaptive Outset AI. Apply feasibility principles, not obsolete platform assumptions. |
| **M2** [GOV.UK: Using in-depth interviews](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/user-research/using-in-depth-interviews) | Map research questions to topics; use neutral starters, simple optional probes and real examples; test guide flow. | Human-interview guidance adapted to AI instructions; not evidence that AI can match human judgment. Do not import generic session-length prescriptions. |
| **M3** [GOV.UK: Find user research participants](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/user-research/find-user-research-participants) | Recruit actual/likely users, specify relevant criteria, include access needs, minimize participant information and use incentives responsibly. | Sample scope must follow the present decision and population, not this page's example participant counts or recruiting timelines. |
| **M4** [GOV.UK: Getting users' consent for research](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/user-research/getting-users-consent-for-research) | Explain purpose, voluntary participation, recordings, processors, sharing, retention, rights/contact and withdrawal; preserve consent evidence. | Public UK guidance is not legal advice or organizational approval. Apply the governing jurisdiction, policy and approved consent process. Add truthful AI disclosure for this mode. |
| **M5** [Government Analysis Function: Questionnaire design guidance](https://analysisfunction.civilservice.gov.uk/policy-store/questionnaire-design-guidance/) | Keep questions clear, balanced and single-focus; address recall, sensitive questions, routing, order, response burden and accessibility; pretest the whole flow in the delivery mode. | Page states it is under review. Apply stable design principles, not every example or a universal scale prescription. Survey recommendations do not make interview responses representative. |
| **M6** [NN/g: AI-Moderated Interviews: If, When, and How to Use Them](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/ai-interviewers/) | Pilot actual AI pacing, repetition, summaries and introduction; retain human judgment, especially for unfamiliar/high-stakes inquiry. | Evaluation involved ten participants and Marvin/UserFlix, **not Outset**. Use findings to identify risks to test, not to claim measured Outset defects or universal AI incapability. |

## Proposed operating rules, not product facts

Treat limited-wave launch, exact-version approval, project-specific probe budgets,
stop/expansion thresholds, human quality review and incident ownership as recommended
research safeguards. Configure them only where supported; otherwise enforce through
the manual workflow or change method. Do not claim a prompt guarantees enforcement.

Preserve the distinction between the vendor's capability descriptions and independent
method advice. NN/g's tested systems and dates differ from current Outset; those
findings do not negate Outset help's description of mode or randomization features.
